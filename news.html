UNIFY News is ready

Two anchors, live weather map, and short-form field reports with voice-over. Turn sound on to hear the anchors (uses your device's voices).

Video library
Field reports

With the news service running (see README) the show pulls the weather plus every local, national and world story from the viewer's own area for the current morning or evening edition, with no cap on how many reports air. Each report gets a generated paper-cut video built from the story's own words: a topic scene, real figures if the text has any, a map with the place pinned, and a title card. Use Save report as video to download one. Without the service you see a sample show.
