## Economics: Supply and demand
### Scarcity and choice
Resources are limited but wants are not, so people and countries must choose. Every choice has an opportunity cost: the next best thing you gave up.
### Prices
- Demand: how much people want to buy at each price. Higher price, less demand.
- Supply: how much sellers want to sell at each price. Higher price, more supply.
- The equilibrium price is where supply meets demand.
Example: if a new phone is scarce and many people want it, the price rises until the number wanted equals the number available.
## Economics: Money and banking
### What money does
Money is a medium of exchange, a way to store value and a way to measure it.
### Banks
- Banks hold deposits and lend part of them out.
- Interest is the price of borrowing money.
- Inflation is a general rise in prices over time.
