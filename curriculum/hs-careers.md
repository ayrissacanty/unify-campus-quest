## Test prep: SAT and ACT strategy
### Know the test
Both tests check reading, writing and math. Check what your target schools want, because many no longer require a test score.
### Preparing well
- Take a practice test first to find your starting point.
- Study your mistakes, not just your score.
- Practice a little each week instead of one big cram.
- Answer every question: there is no penalty for guessing.
## Applications: Essays and resumes
### The personal essay
Show one real moment in detail instead of listing achievements. Admissions readers remember stories.
### Resumes
- One page, simple layout, verbs first (led, built, organized).
- Include numbers where you can: "raised $400 for the food bank".
- Proofread twice and ask someone else to read it.

- A personal essay shows one real moment in detail

check: sort Which line is stronger on a resume?
- Stronger: Organized a canned-food drive that collected 300 cans | Led a team of 6 volunteers
- Weaker: Responsible for helping with things | Was part of a club

- resume: A one-page summary of your experience, skills and achievements.
- personal essay: A short true story that shows who you are.
- action verb: A strong verb that starts a line, such as led, built or organized.

- Numbers let readers see the size of what you did.
- A specific moment is easier to remember than a general claim.
