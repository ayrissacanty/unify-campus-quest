## Study skills: Notes and review
### Taking notes
- Write short phrases, not whole sentences.
- Leave space. Add questions in the margin.
- Within a day, add what you missed.
### Reviewing
Review within 24 hours, then again after a few days, then after a week. Each review takes less time, and the memory lasts longer.
## Study skills: Planning your week
### Plan backwards
Start from the due date, split the work into small tasks and put each on a day. Do the hard thing when you have the most energy.
### Avoiding overload
- Pick three priorities per day.
- Study in 25 minute blocks with 5 minute breaks.
- Phone out of reach while you work.

@lesson Study Skills: Planning your week
blurb: Plan backwards from deadlines.
intro: Today we plan our week so deadlines don't sneak up on us.
points:
- Start with the due date and plan backwards
- Break big tasks into small ones
- Pick three priorities a day
- Work in 25 minute blocks with short breaks
examples:
- Essay due Friday: Monday outline, Tuesday draft, Wednesday edit, Thursday polish
- Phone in another room during your 25 minute block
- Do the hardest task when you have the most energy
script:
- say[smile]: Welcome. A plan is a gift from you today to you on a busy Thursday.
- board[left] Plan backwards: Write the due date | Split the work | Put each part on a day | Pick three priorities
- say[explain]: Big tasks feel scary because they have no shape. Cut them into pieces and each piece feels easy.
- ask
- say[thinking]: Protect your breaks too. Rest is part of the plan, not a reward after it.
- read: Study skills: Planning your week/Plan backwards
check: order Plan an essay due Friday, backwards.
- Monday: outline
- Tuesday: first draft
- Wednesday: edit
- Thursday: polish and proofread
- Friday: hand it in
glossary:
- deadline: The time by which something must be finished.
- priority: Something that is more important than other things right now.
- procrastination: Putting off a task until later.
whys:
- Small tasks are easier to start, and starting is the hardest part.
- Planning backwards makes sure the last day is calm.
wrap: Plan backwards, keep tasks small, protect your breaks.
homework: Plan your next big assignment backwards from its due date.
